Case study / 01

Enterprise AI intake & governance.

Azure AI Foundry   ·   Azure OpenAI
AI agents   ·   Enterprise governance

01The challenge

Enterprise AI opportunities need a consistent process for evaluating business impact, technical feasibility, and potential AI risks, while coordinating requests across stakeholders.

02My contribution

Established enterprise AI governance frameworks, security controls, and architecture roadmaps across Azure, Databricks, and Microsoft Copilot. Developed an AI intake and prioritization web application using Azure AI Foundry and Azure OpenAI.

03The solution

Designed and implemented an agentic AI workflow for intake, registration, and assignment of AI requests. The intake application also supports structured evaluation of business impact, technical feasibility, and AI risks.

04Why it matters

The work connects enterprise AI strategy with practical workflow automation, supporting consistent reviews, request coordination, and responsible AI adoption.

Workflow shown above is illustrative, not an internal system diagram. Details are limited to experience shared for this portfolio.
